Activities

Sasthra Vedhi conducts a wide range of programmes that connect science, technology, education and society. Our activities are designed to encourage scientific thinking, expand access to knowledge and create opportunities for learning and public engagement.

Science & Technology Programmes

We organise lectures, seminars, discussions and interactive programmes covering developments in science and technology and their relevance to society.

These programmes provide opportunities for students, educators, professionals, researchers and the public to exchange knowledge and ideas.

Student & Youth Programmes

Young people are an important part of Sasthra Vedhi's activities.

We conduct science camps, quizzes, competitions, awareness programmes, learning sessions and other initiatives that encourage curiosity, creativity and interest in science and technology.

Through these programmes, we aim to create opportunities for young people to explore ideas beyond the conventional classroom.

Science Communication

Making scientific knowledge accessible to the wider public is an important part of our work.

Through talks, articles, publications, online programmes and public discussions, we seek to communicate scientific concepts and developments in a clear and accessible manner.

Environment & Sustainability

Science and technology have an important role in understanding and addressing environmental challenges.

Sasthra Vedhi conducts programmes and discussions related to environmental protection, climate change, energy, natural resources, sustainability and other issues affecting our environment and communities.

Innovation & Critical Thinking

We encourage people to look at problems from different perspectives and explore creative solutions.

Our programmes promote questioning, logical reasoning, problem-solving and an openness to new ideas.

Public Awareness

Scientific knowledge can help communities understand many issues that directly affect everyday life.

We conduct awareness programmes addressing contemporary social and public-interest issues, encouraging informed discussion and responsible decision-making.

Publications & Knowledge Sharing

Sasthra Vedhi supports the creation and distribution of science-related knowledge through its magazine, publications, articles and other educational resources.

These efforts help bring scientific ideas and discussions to a wider audience.

Community Engagement

Our activities extend beyond formal educational institutions.

Through district-level programmes and community initiatives, Sasthra Vedhi works to create opportunities for people from different backgrounds to participate in science-related learning, discussion and social engagement.

Building a Culture of Inquiry

Across all our activities, the underlying objective remains the same:

To encourage people to ask questions, seek evidence, share knowledge and approach the challenges of the future with scientific thinking.
