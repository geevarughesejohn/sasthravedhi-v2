About Sasthra Vedhi

Sasthra Vedhi (ശാസ്ത്രവേദി) is a platform dedicated to advancing scientific thinking, knowledge and the responsible application of science and technology for the benefit of society.

We believe that science belongs to everyone. It is not confined to laboratories, classrooms or academic institutions. Scientific thinking helps individuals and communities understand the world around them, question assumptions, examine evidence and make informed decisions.

Sasthra Vedhi works to create a meaningful connection between science, technology and society. It brings together people from different backgrounds who share an interest in science, knowledge, innovation and social progress.

Our work encompasses science communication, public engagement, education and discussions on matters where science and technology have an important role in shaping society. We seek to create opportunities for students, teachers, researchers, professionals and the public to learn, exchange ideas and participate in conversations about the scientific and technological challenges of our time.

Sasthra Vedhi also recognises that scientific and technological progress must be considered alongside its social and environmental implications. We therefore encourage informed dialogue on issues affecting the future of our communities and the wider society.

At its heart, Sasthra Vedhi is about curiosity, inquiry, knowledge and constructive dialogue.

We seek to build a culture where people are encouraged to ask questions, respect evidence, explore new ideas and approach the challenges of the future with knowledge and reason.

Our Identity

Sasthra Vedhi brings together a diverse community of people connected by a shared interest in science and society. Through its state-level organisation and district-level presence, it seeks to provide a platform for interaction, learning and public engagement.

The organisation's programmes, publications and initiatives reflect its continuing effort to make scientific knowledge more accessible and relevant to everyday life.

Towards a Scientific Society

The challenges facing society are increasingly interconnected with science and technology. Climate change, energy, healthcare, education, artificial intelligence, environmental sustainability and emerging technologies all require informed public discussion.

Sasthra Vedhi seeks to contribute to that discussion by encouraging scientific temper, critical inquiry and responsible engagement with knowledge.

We believe that a society that asks questions, values evidence and remains open to learning is better equipped to understand and respond to the challenges of the future.
