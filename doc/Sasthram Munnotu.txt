Sasthram Munnotu
A Magazine for Science, Knowledge and Society

Sasthram Munnotu (ശാസ്ത്രം മുന്നോട്ട്) is the magazine of Sasthra Vedhi, created to bring science, technology and contemporary knowledge closer to the wider public.

The magazine provides a platform for informed discussion on developments in science and technology and their relationship with society, environment and everyday life.

What We Cover

Sasthram Munnotu features articles and discussions on a wide range of subjects, including:

Science and technology

Environment and sustainability

Health and scientific developments

Education and learning

Innovation and emerging technologies

Artificial intelligence and digital technology

Energy and development

Science, society and public policy

Contemporary scientific issues

Ideas and perspectives from researchers, educators and experts

Connecting Science with Society

Scientific developments increasingly influence the way we live, work and understand the world.

Sasthram Munnotu seeks to place these developments in a wider context—explaining scientific ideas while also exploring their social, environmental and practical implications.

The magazine encourages readers to look beyond headlines, ask questions and engage with issues through knowledge and evidence.

A Platform for Ideas

The magazine also provides an opportunity for scientists, academics, teachers, students, professionals and science enthusiasts to share their knowledge and perspectives.

Through accessible writing and informed discussion, Sasthram Munnotu aims to encourage a culture of curiosity, critical thinking and continuous learning.

Read. Question. Understand.

In a rapidly changing world, staying informed requires more than simply receiving information.

Sasthram Munnotu invites readers to read with curiosity, question with reason and understand with an open mind.
